## Implications for MoveAhead

- ✅ Suitable for object tracking (`sv.ByteTrack`), detection post-processing, video annotation, visualisation, and evaluation.
- ✅ Can be integrated into proprietary commercial applications without disclosing MoveAhead's source code.
- ✅ Internal-only use generally requires no public attribution.
- ⚠️ Detector models (YOLO11, RF-DETR, etc.), pretrained weights, dependencies, and datasets have separate licensing requirements.
